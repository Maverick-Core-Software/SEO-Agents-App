We get asked all the time: "Do I really need an interlock, or can I just plug my generator into the dryer outlet?"

**BODY:**
Short answer: no. Backfeeding through a dryer outlet sends power back onto the grid, and that's dangerous for the crews working to restore your neighborhood. An interlock kit physically prevents the main breaker and the generator breaker from being on at the same time. We install them for homes in Rowlett, Rockwall, and Garland. It's not the flashiest upgrade, but it's the one that keeps everyone safe.
